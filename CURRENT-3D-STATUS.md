The retained parameters are P=89.371ms, M=1.4 solar masses, R=13km, polar B=3e12G, magnetic tilt75deg and Tinf=0.70MK. The existing approximate atmosphere supplies the stellar radiation with Schwarzschild surface rays and local rotational Doppler effects. No new atmosphere, particle or PIC evolution was run. No Vela fitting is required or claimed

The luminous curves are prescribed near-zone dipole geometry; their visibility is illustrative. The polar envelopes are directional guides with an assumed geometry-based width, Gaussian profile and graphical length. They are not literal photon paths, plasma jets or quantitative optical-emission predictions. Their light is not calibrated against the star's physical radiance. Only the star uses the relativistic surface camera; the guide projection is Euclidean

All 18 focused geometry and retained surface-camera tests pass. Measured delivery checks pass: 1080x1920, 30fps, 960frames, 32seconds, H.264 High, yuv420p, Rec.709 limited range and no audio. Four decoded eight-second cycles are identical. The geometric transform and image endpoints are periodic; decoded seam motion is comparable with ordinary neighboring frames
