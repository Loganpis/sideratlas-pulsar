NO.002 — THE PULSAR
@sideratlas
Vela visible-light model — research in progress
Visible band: 400–700 nm · Final simulation and Reel not yet available

Rotating electromagnetic fields accelerate electron–positron plasma
Optical radiation depends on particle energies and pitch angles
Relativistic beaming changes the light directed toward the observer
Light-travel time changes when photons arrive
t_obs = t − n·r/c (far observer, flat spacetime)
Iν,obs = g³ Iν,em (surface frequency-intensity invariant)
R_LC = cP/(2π) ≈ 4,264 km; P ≈ 0.08937 s
Fν ∝ ν^(−α); measured optical α = −0.04 ± 0.04
