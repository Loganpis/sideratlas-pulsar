NO.002 — THE PULSAR
@sideratlas
Original optical model — rotation-powered pulsar
Vela provides physical reference parameters, not a fitted target
P = 89.37 ms · M = 1.4 M☉ · R = 13 km
B_p = 3×10¹² G · T∞ = 0.70 MK
Magnetic inclination 75° · Observer 65°
Magnetic fields steer charged particles
Accelerated pairs emit optical harmonics
Radiation cools their transverse motion
Rotation sweeps pulsed light past the observer
Gravity bends and redshifts surface light
ν_n = n eB/[2πγm_e(1−β∥cosθ)]
τ = t−n·r/c · Optical band: 400–700 nm
CALCULATED
Guiding-centre paths, cooling and directional harmonic emission
Photon Doppler shifts, aberration and relative arrival times
Two-mode LTE atmosphere transfer and Schwarzschild surface rays
CIE1931 spectral color with fixed exposure and sRGB tone mapping
ASSUMED
Prescribed near-zone dipole and pair supply, separate from local PIC
Pairs activated at 0.08–0.18 R_LC with γ=300, pitch=0.1 rad
6000 pairs per primary; cap annulus 0.8–0.9; both poles
Uniform normal-field, fully ionized H surface; unity Gaunt factors
No cascade, radio heating, plasma absorption or extinction
No atomic opacity, dispersion or vacuum mode conversion
Spherical surface; no frame dragging or GR plasma transport
Finite emitting cells and pulse sampling; surface grid sensitivity ≈5%
One rotation slowed to 8 seconds, repeated four times
Display exposure enhanced; no naked-eye or exact Vela prediction
