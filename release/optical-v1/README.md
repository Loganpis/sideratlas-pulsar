# SiderAtlas NO.002 — THE PULSAR

A calculated optical visualization of a plausible young rotation-powered pulsar, with Vela as a physical reference. It combines prescribed-field pair transport and directional harmonic radiation with an approximate magnetic hydrogen atmosphere and relativistic stellar camera. It is not a fitted Vela reconstruction or self-consistent global optical PIC simulation.

## Release contents

- `instagram-description.text` — ready-to-copy caption under Instagram's 2200-character limit
- `MODEL-NOTES.md` — inputs, equations, assumptions, numerical evidence and full source roles
- [Final Reel](../../media/optical/sideratlas-no002-optical-32s.mp4) and [measured export receipt](verification.json) — 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 32 seconds, one eight-second rotation repeated four times

The full portrait canvas uses a black background, Latin Modern typography and only `@sideratlas`, `THE PULSAR` and `NO.002`. Spectral optical color uses fixed enhanced exposure and common tone mapping; it is not a naked-eye brightness prediction.

The native coupled PIC study remains separate research. Its reduced particles do not supply physical optical photons. The selected atmosphere passes numerical balance gates but retains approximately 5% depth-grid sensitivity and substantive ionization/opacity assumptions, documented in the notes.

Code and reports are in `research/optical-finish/`, `research/pic-source-repair/` and matching `outputs/` folders. Large raw datasets and unlicensed downloads are excluded from Git. The export receipt confirms 960 frames, Rec.709 limited-range H.264, no audio and identical decoded repeated cycles.
